# Advanced Fastify Interview Questions – Senior Engineer Prep

This page contains advanced, interview-style questions for senior backend engineers.

---

## 31. What is the difference between Fastify and Express at the architectural level?

Fastify is more schema-driven and plugin-centric, while Express is more minimal and unopinionated. Fastify is usually better suited for high-performance and strongly validated APIs.

---

## 32. When would you choose Fastify over NestJS or Express?

You would choose Fastify when you want:
- high throughput
- a lightweight core
- strong validation and serialization
- plugin-based architecture

NestJS may be a better fit when you want a structured enterprise framework with decorators and dependency injection.

---

## 33. How do you avoid route duplication in Fastify?

You can avoid duplication by:
- grouping related routes in plugins
- using shared services
- centralizing validation schemas
- reusing decorators and helpers

---

## 34. How would you design a production-grade Fastify service?

A production-grade Fastify service should have:
- modular plugins
- clear separation of concerns
- centralized error handling
- validation schemas
- structured logging
- monitoring and health checks

---

## 35. How would you answer if asked, “Why should a team adopt Fastify?”

I would say Fastify is a strong option when teams need performance, maintainability, and modern API engineering practices. It combines speed with a clean plugin architecture and built-in validation, which reduces boilerplate and improves reliability.

---

## Bonus senior interview tip
When asked about Fastify in an interview, speak not only about syntax but also about:
- architecture
- plugin design
- performance tradeoffs
- validation strategy
- production readiness

---

## Final summary
Fastify is a strong choice for senior backend engineering because it helps teams build performant, scalable, and well-structured APIs while keeping the framework lightweight and developer-friendly.
