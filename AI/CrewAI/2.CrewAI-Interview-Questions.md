# CrewAI Interview Questions

## 1. What is CrewAI?
CrewAI is a framework for orchestrating multiple AI agents to collaborate on tasks.

## 2. Why use CrewAI?
It helps split work across specialized roles such as researcher, writer, and reviewer.

## 3. What are the main building blocks?
Agents, tasks, and workflows.

## 4. What is a common use case?
Research and content generation pipelines.

## 5. What is the main advantage?
It makes multi-agent collaboration easier to manage and reason about.
